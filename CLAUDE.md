# drako! TikTok marketing memory

This repo is a fork of TikTok-Api. This file is the persistent memory for helping **drako! (@drakoo.wav)** grow their TikTok and music. Read it at the start of every session.

## Who they are
- Underground rapper and producer from Norfolk / Virginia Beach, VA. Makes beats in FL Studio and raps on their own songs.
- Account plan: mostly opinion text posts (photo mode), plus videos they want to post (beats, music).
- Goal: turn views into **profile views, followers and listens to their music**.
- Release plan (as of Oct 26, 2026): a **project drops the week of Oct 26**. A separate unreleased single (its snippet was planned as the sound on the "if y'all gonna hate on Jay-Z I should get the Super Bowl" post; the song's name is unknown) is **not on the project** and comes out after it.

## Standing instructions
- Before writing any new post, review the analytics below (and any new screenshots) and say what's working and what's holding them back. Keep the good habits and drop the bad ones.
- When they send new weekly analytics, add them to the tables below, then commit and push to `main`.
- Use ALL available resources on every task: TikTok-Api (hashtag stats, post stats), web search, the MarketingSkills `social` skill, and the data in this file. Don't just finish tasks, aim for posts that perform like the best ones here. If a tool is blocked, say so and use the next best source.

- **Be a check-and-balance partner, not a yes-man.** Always consider their ideas seriously, but if the stats or likely fan reactions say an idea will hurt, say so clearly, explain why with the data, and give the better option.

- **Check with them before making any change they didn't directly ask for**, even small ones (swapping a hashtag, adding a line, editing this file). Suggest it and wait for a yes.

## Writing rules (their preferences)
- **Always think through the aftermath of every line before suggesting it.** Ask how fans will react and what they'll do next (stream, wait, skip, follow), then pick the option that best builds anticipation and long-term growth as an artist.
- **Photo/text post** = screen text + title (bold line at the bottom) + caption. **Video posts have no title**; the first line of the caption does that job.
- Screen text: a strong line, then a twist in brackets, often with 😭. Vary the twist; repeated "DON'T say" / "NOT ___" lines got stale. The bracket can just be funny; it doesn't always need to invite a response.
- **No made-up details.** Only use facts they gave or that are verifiably true. Production details must come from them.
- **No obvious engagement bait** in captions ("comment below", "full song on my page", "thoughts?"). They delete it.
- Always use the **strongest buzzwords** for the topic in titles and captions.
- Max **5 hashtags** (TikTok limit). No #fyp / #viral.
- Celebrity hot takes: aim the joke at the person who did something, not at anyone's looks.
- Sound: **their own song** by default (their top post used it). Use their beat on posts aimed at artists, with the sound renamed to e.g. `prod drako` (TikTok sound names can't contain hyphens or "!", so leave out punctuation).
- Location: Norfolk · Virginia Beach.
- **No movie/TV spoilers** in answers or post copy (e.g. Avengers: Doomsday). Release date and casting are fine; plot, trailer moments and leaks are not.

## Post goals by type
- **Song posts → get people to stream it.** Hashtags for listeners/rap fans (#rapper, #undergroundrap, artists the song sounds like); no producer tags. Use the official distributed version of the song as the sound when possible (sound page can link to streaming apps). Song title + name in the caption's first line and a pinned comment so it's searchable. Strongest moment in the first 1–2 seconds; show their face when possible.
- **Beat posts → reach artists looking for producers.** Hashtags for artists (#rapper, #undergroundrap, artists the beat sounds like); no #producertok/#flstudio. Overlay speaks to artists ("Artists:"). Sound name like `[artist] type beat prod drako`. Have a ready reply for "how do I get this?".
- Conversation/text posts that carry their song count as song posts for hashtag purposes when the goal is listens.

## What works / what doesn't
- Works: one clear question or hot take plus a bracket twist on screen; producer knowledge questions (lots of saves); news hot takes (views and comments); replying in comments.
- Doesn't: posts with no question or take; two questions at once; asking people to do something for you; running the same topic many posts in a row.
- Posting mix (agreed Oct 27): about 2–3 conversation posts for every 1 music/face post. Conversation posts should pull hip-hop/music fans (the audience that fits their music) and always carry their song as the sound. Space same-topic posts at least a day apart.
- Biggest gap: text posts get views but few followers. Posts showing their face or music convert far better. Fixes: pin posts on the profile, a clear bio, video replies on camera, a face/music post every 3–4 posts, series ("part 2").

## TikTok-Api notes
- Run from repo root with `PYTHONPATH=.`; launch Chromium with `executable_path="/opt/pw-browsers/chromium"`.
- Without an `ms_token`, TikTok often returns empty responses. Hashtag lookups work intermittently (one session per tag, with pauses). User profile listing fails.
- Video lookups need the full URL (`https://www.tiktok.com/@drakoo.wav/video/<id>`). Resolve short links with `curl -sIL -o /dev/null -w '%{url_effective}'`; photo-post IDs work with `/video/` in the URL.
